## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: **Astro** (static output). Chosen because the site is multi-page, content-driven and franchise-shaped: units, disciplines, courses and trips live well as content collections, so adding a new franchise unit is a data entry rather than a new page build; campaign landing pages share components with the institutional site; output is plain static HTML with minimal JS, which keeps pages fast on older phones. Deploy target and form backend are still undecided (see Capabilities and Constraints).

## Users

- **Pessoas 60+** in Porto Alegre and Pelotas (and future franchise cities) who want to keep learning and meeting people. The strategy splits them by need, not age: the *curioso cultural* ("will this be too basic?"), the person looking for a *new weekly routine* ("will I feel at ease?"), the *social explorer* ("I don't want something that feels like elder care"). They range from 61 and still working to 80+ with some mobility limits. Most use WhatsApp daily; a share is less comfortable online.
- **Adult children and relatives (filho/filha-ponte)** looking for an experience to suggest or gift to a parent. A hypothesis to be tested (for example, "Estou me inscrevendo para mim / Quero convidar ou presentear alguém"), not an assumption. The 60+ person stays the protagonist.
- **Prospective franchisees**: implied by the franchise model. Not a confirmed audience for this build.

## Product Purpose

CICLOS+ is a *Escola Cultural 60+* ("Aprender, conectar, viver!"): ongoing cultural education for people over 60, with no exams and no pressure, paired with a real social life around it. The site must:

1. Explain in seconds what CICLOS+ is: a cultural school where people 60+ learn art, philosophy, film, history, music and more, surrounded by good conversation and new experiences.
2. Present the whole brand and its units (Porto Alegre, Pelotas, and more to come).
3. Give each offering its own page: disciplinas semestrais, cursos livres, viagens culturais, confraternizações.
4. Host the Pelotas campaign landing pages, which carry one funnel toward enrollment in March 2027:
   - **Masterclass (outubro)**: the "Experimente" step.
   - **4 aulas livres / cursos livres (novembro)**: the "Encontre seu assunto" step.
   - **Inscrição nas disciplinas semestrais de Pelotas (março 2027)**: priority list, then enrollment.

Success means leads that are qualified and attributed, and that turn into enrollments. Reach alone does not count.

## Positioning

CICLOS+ does not compete on "activities for seniors". UCPel, UFPel, Sesc and municipal programs offer that, often for free. It owns the ground of **vida cultural continuada**: professor + curadoria + assunto + grupo + conversa + rotina + cidade + encontros + viagens + novas referências. It is a small, curated, boutique school: depth without academicism, continuity across semesters, a stable community, and experiences beyond the classroom (museums, theatre, trips). The mental shift it aims for: from "curso para idosos" to "a escola cultural que eu gostaria de frequentar nesta fase da vida".

Internal line: *"Ciclos+ é a escola cultural de quem acredita que uma vida interessante continua cheia de assuntos, pessoas e experiências novas."* Campaign concept (Pelotas): *"Seu próximo assunto favorito começa aqui."* / *"A curiosidade continua."*

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Conversion:** a short form (interest, priority list, event sign-up) is the primary action. WhatsApp is always there as the human alternative. Forms must be short, and payment must never depend only on an app.
- **Units are data:** the architecture must let new franchise units be added without redesign. Each unit can carry its own address, contact, schedule and local campaigns.
- **Campaign pages are temporary but branded:** they sit under the institutional brand and hand off to it.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Official WhatsApp number(s). The current site uses two conflicting numbers, (51) 99343-3639 and (51) 98153-0001. Pending confirmation, including whether each unit gets its own.
  - Form backend / CRM destination, and deploy target.
  - Masterclass theme, date, price and venue. The strategy suggests options; none is confirmed.
  - The 4 November class topics, dates and the "Passaporte Novembro" mechanic.
  - 2027 Pelotas grid, prices and teachers.
  - Newsletter: keep or drop.
  - Media section content (links and view counts in the current site are dated).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **CICLOS+** (always this form, never "Ciclos", "Ciclomais" or "Ciclos Mais" in copy). Descriptor: *Escola Cultural 60+*. Tagline: *Aprender, conectar, viver!*
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese. Prefer "pessoas 60+", "alunos", "maturidade", "você". Avoid making "idoso" or "terceira idade" the dominant identity.
- Never a health or deficit pitch ("combater solidão/declínio cognitivo"). Always desire, curiosity, protagonism.
- Pelotas must never be framed as "chegando" or relaunched. Use: "A Ciclos+ já faz parte de Pelotas."
- Represent people 60+ as protagonist adults: looking at art, debating, travelling, painting, singing, laughing with friends. Never as frail people being cared for.
- Existing logo/wordmark: not yet in the project folder.

## Product Principles

1. **Cultural school first, age second.** Lead with the subject and the experience. "Escola Cultural 60+" appears as context, not as the pitch.
2. **Make the difference concrete.** Show a specific afternoon (a film debated, a façade read, a painting understood, a trip taken) rather than generic "bem-estar e conexão".
3. **Always a human in reach.** Every path has an easy, visible way to talk to a person. No dead-end forms.
4. **One funnel, not isolated events.** Campaign pages move people to the next step (Masterclass → aulas de novembro → lista 2027 → matrícula) and record where each lead came from.
5. **Local, not imported.** Each unit speaks to its own city. Pelotas talks about Pelotas: its heritage, the Sete de Abril, its casarões.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience is 60+, with varied vision, dexterity and digital familiarity. Target WCAG 2.2 AA as a floor: large, legible body type; strong contrast; generous tap targets; no information carried by hover or motion alone; captions on video; plain, explicit CTAs; forms with few fields, visible labels and clear errors; phone and WhatsApp as equal alternatives to any digital flow; respect for reduced motion.
